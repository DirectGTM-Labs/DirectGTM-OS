# DirectGTM-OS
The Open-Source Gatekeeper-Bypass Pipeline for GTM Engineers, Growth Engineers, Growth Hackers.

DirectGTM-OS is an open-source Growth and GTM engineering system for turning signals, research, data, and automation into direct opportunities.

It was originally created from a simple premise:

> **If traditional systems act as gatekeepers between you and an opportunity, build your own acquisition system.**

Instead of relying entirely on passive funnels, ATS filtering, intermediaries, or platform-driven distribution, DirectGTM-OS treats opportunity acquisition as a product-led GTM problem:

**Identify → Research → Detect Signals → Qualify → Engage → Automate → Measure → Learn**

The first use case is **career acquisition**: applying GTM, Growth Hacking, and Growth Engineering principles to acquire professional opportunities directly.

The underlying system, however, is designed to generalize beyond careers to consulting acquisition, B2B prospecting, lead generation, outbound GTM, and other signal-driven growth workflows.

## What DirectGTM-OS Demonstrates

This project is also a practical demonstration of how a Growth Engineer approaches problems:

- **GTM:** identifying markets, ICPs, accounts, and opportunities
    
- **Growth Hacking:** finding alternative paths around inefficient or restrictive acquisition funnels
    
- **Engineering:** turning repeatable growth processes into systems and workflows
    
- **Data:** collecting, structuring, scoring, and analysing signals
    
- **Automation:** reducing repetitive research and outreach operations
    
- **Experimentation:** testing acquisition hypotheses and measuring outcomes
    
- **AI / GEO:** using semantic and AI-assisted research where it creates a measurable advantage
    
- **Measurement:** connecting actions to conversion, learning, and iteration
    

DirectGTM-OS is therefore not intended to be merely a job-search tool.

It is a **living Growth Engineering project** that uses career acquisition as its initial dogfooding environment.

## Why "Gatekeeper Bypass"?

"Gatekeeper bypass" does not mean circumventing security, access controls, or legitimate rules.

It means reducing unnecessary dependence on intermediaries in acquisition.

Instead of asking:

> _How do I get selected by the system?_

DirectGTM-OS asks:

> _How can I identify the right opportunity, reach the right person, create relevance, and measure the outcome directly?_

That distinction is the foundation of the project.

## From Career Acquisition to GTM Engineering

The project begins with a personal experiment:

**Can a Growth Engineer apply GTM principles to acquire their own opportunities?**

The broader hypothesis is:

> **If the acquisition system works on yourself, the underlying methodology can potentially be generalized to acquiring customers, leads, consulting opportunities, partnerships, and other forms of demand.**

This makes DirectGTM-OS both a practical system and an ongoing Growth Engineering experiment.

## Project Philosophy

**Don't just learn Growth. Engineer it.**

Build the system.

Run the experiment.

Measure the result.

Document what works.

Then turn the learning into a reusable system.
