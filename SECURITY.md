# Security reports

DirectGTM-Labs is an early-stage independent laboratory. There is no stable
production service or guaranteed security-response SLA declared by this policy.

For a suspected vulnerability or exposed secret, email
contact@mingmingcodes.com privately. Include the repository, affected commit
or version, a concise description, potential impact and a minimal reproduction
using synthetic data when possible.

Do not post credentials, exploit details affecting others, private contact
records or confidential client information in a public issue. Do not test a
third-party service without authorisation.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis. No bounty or supported-version
schedule is currently promised. Each repository may publish a more specific
security policy as its implementation matures.
